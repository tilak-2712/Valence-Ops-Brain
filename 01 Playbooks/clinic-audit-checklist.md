Fill one of these per clinic, before writing a single word of outreach. **Budget 10 minutes for the Day-0 fields (§0–§3, §6). ## Go / No-Go

- [ ] I have at least **one** specific, dated, factual hook — not a compliment, not a guess.
- [ ] **The hook survives the rebuttal test** — I wrote the sentence the doctor says back, and there isn't a plausible one. *(`wedge-signal-entry.md` §0.1 — this is the gate that actually matters.)*
- [ ] **If the hook rests on a mystery shop, the enquiry landed inside the clinic's own published Google hours.**
- [ ] The hook is either a first-party test or a pattern across ≥2 independent instances — not a single review quote.
- [ ] I know which **single** wedge from `wedge-signal-entry.md` §3 this hook points to.
- [ ] The CTA follows from the hook. *(A hook about response speed and an ask about no-shows is two messages stapled together — this has already shipped once.)*
- [ ] A named decision-maker is reachable on a personal channel. *(Hard kill #10 — if not, stop here.)*

If any box above is unchecked, the audit isn't done. Don't send yet.
