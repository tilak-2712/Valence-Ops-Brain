## Page 1 — What we do, and what we don't

Most of what gets sold to clinics is about getting more people to enquire. More ads, better ads, more followers, a new website.

We don't do any of that. If your problem is that not enough people are messaging you, we're the wrong people and we'd tell you so.

**What we work on is what happens after someone enquires.**

Somebody messages your WhatsApp at nine on a Saturday night asking about laser. Somebody books a consult for Thursday and doesn't turn up. Somebody comes in, gets a price for a package, says they'll think about it — and nobody follows up, because your front desk is dealing with the people physically standing in front of them.

None of that appears in a report. There's no line anywhere that says *"eleven people stopped at the quote stage this month."* The patient simply doesn't come back, and nobody at the clinic knows it happened.

**Three things we do:**

**Reply when nobody's there.** So a message at 11pm doesn't sit until morning — and more usefully, so that whoever calls back the next day already knows which treatment the person asked about, roughly when they're thinking of doing it, and which branch suits them. Right now that call starts from nothing.

**Follow up on what's already in motion.** Consults booked and not attended. Quotes given and gone quiet. Treatment courses where the second session never got scheduled. These are people who already chose you once.

**Bring back patients you've stopped hearing from.** The ones who came twice a year and then didn't.

**Three things we don't do:**

- We don't do marketing, ads, or getting you more enquiries.
- We don't replace anyone on your team. Everything we build hands over to your coordinator the moment a patient says something a system shouldn't be answering.
- We don't give medical advice or let anything we build near a clinical question. Anything of that kind goes to a person immediately.

## Page 2 — How it actually works

**Step 1 — A conversation. About 30 minutes.**

We ask questions about how an enquiry moves through your clinic. What happens when someone messages. What happens when they don't turn up. Where your coordinator keeps track of who still needs calling.

We don't pitch on this call. There's nothing to pitch yet — we don't know enough about your clinic to say anything useful.

**Step 2 — A short document. Two or three days later.**

What we heard, in your words. What we could confirm from outside. What we still can't see. The three things that appear to be costing you the most, ranked. And which one we'd fix first, and why that one.

You read it and decide. Plenty of clinics have read it and done nothing, and that's a fine outcome — the document is worth having either way.

**Step 3 — One thing, built and run for two to three weeks.**

Not everything. One thing, so you can see whether it does anything before your team changes how they work.

We'll show you every message before it goes out. Nothing sends without your approval. Anyone who replies goes straight to your coordinator — not to us.

**Step 4 — We look at what happened, together.**

Booked before we start, so it's in the calendar rather than left open. We go through what worked, what didn't, and what it showed about your clinic that wasn't visible before. Then you decide whether to carry on.

**What we need from you**

Less than you'd expect, but the second one is not negotiable:

1. Whatever record you already have — a sheet, a CRM export, or a photo of the register. However it exists is fine. We don't need it cleaned up.
2. **One person we can reach who replies within a few working hours** when a patient responds and something needs a decision.
3. About half an hour to approve the wording before anything goes out.

The second one is the whole thing. If a patient replies and nobody at your clinic gets back to them, this doesn't work — and it leaves that patient worse off than if we'd never messaged. If that isn't going to be possible in a given month, we'd rather wait than run it badly.

**What we never do with your data**

Patient information stays in your systems. We don't move it anywhere else, we don't keep a copy after a pilot ends, and nothing goes to any third party. Everything we build sends from your clinic's own number and lands back with your own team.

**Building and running the system is paid.** That starts only after you've read the document and decided you want it.

**What we won't promise you**

We won't tell you this will bring you a certain number of patients. Anyone who gives you that number is guessing, and the guess is usually chosen to be the one you want to hear.

**What we will promise:** if the free period produces nothing, you owe nothing, and we'll show you exactly where it stalled — which list, which message, at what point people stopped replying. That's genuinely useful to you whether or not you ever work with us, and it's the only thing we can honestly commit to before we've started.
