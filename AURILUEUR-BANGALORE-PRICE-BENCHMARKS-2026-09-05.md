# Bangalore Published Price Benchmarks — 11 Treatment Categories

**Compiled 5 September 2026.** Companion to `AURILUEUR-REVENUE-RESEARCH-2026-09-05.md` §9. Aurilueur's own pages were **excluded** from this research so the comparison stays independent.

**Confidence key:** `published-bangalore` = a Bangalore clinic's own page or a Bangalore-specific guide · `published-india` = national aggregator or multi-city page · `press` = news/market reporting · `not-published` = no real published price found.

**Headline caveat, and it matters for the model:** almost no Bangalore clinic publishes a **CoolSculpting course total.** The published unit is per-session or per-area. Any course total in our model is **derived, not sourced.**

## Quality audit

**41 sourced rows:** 30 `published-bangalore` · 8 `published-india` · 3 `press` · 1 category `not-published`.

**Dropped, and why:** ClinicSpots CoolSculpting pages (returned navigation chrome, no prices — do not cite); Sakhiya cryolipolysis and Venkat Center hair transplant (HTTP 403, unread); Aesthetic International Bangalore lymphatic (connection dropped); Kaya body contouring Bangalore (price table exists but every cell is a "Get Price" link — genuinely `not-published`); all Aurilueur pages (target clinic, excluded by design).

**Promotional floors, flagged and not treated as averages:** Charma GFC ₹8,500 · Charma peel ₹3,500 · Dr Dixit GFC ₹5,000 · Oliva LHR "from ₹2,500" · Oliva "up to 35% off" on HIFU and PDRN · Reborn Hyderabad ₹5,999/probe · Calyx "Reset from ₹79,999".

**Unit-confidence flags:** HexaHealth CoolSculpting Bangalore (unit unstated — the largest single ambiguity in the set) · Dr Dixit fillers ₹5,000–45,000 (unit unstated) · Pristyn Care laser vaginal tightening (labelled full-procedure but the same page says session count varies).

**No real published Bangalore pricing found for:**
1. A **CoolSculpting course total** from any competitor — market publishes per-session only
2. **HIFU body** priced separately from face
3. **Lymphatic drainage as an aesthetic add-on** — only physiotherapy rates exist, a different service at a different price point
4. **Vaginoplasty from a Bangalore clinic** — aggregators only, mutually contradictory
