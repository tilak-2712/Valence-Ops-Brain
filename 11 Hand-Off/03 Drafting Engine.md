# THE DRAFTING ENGINE — how to write to these clinics

Everything needed to draft a real message from a phone. **Consistency in the thinking, variation in
the wording.**

> **Scope: this file governs words that reach a clinic** — a DM, an email, a WhatsApp message, a
> document, a call script. **It does not govern internal thinking, analysis or strategy talk.** When
> working out pricing, channel mix or positioning, use whatever vocabulary is precise: funnel,
> conversion rate, CAC, payback, retention are all the right words in that context. The banned list
> below is about not sounding like an agency *to a doctor* — it is not a thinking constraint.

> **Hard rule: nothing goes into a draft that isn't a verified fact about that clinic.** No invented
> details, no assumed pain points, no "probably." If a fact is missing, ask for it or flag the gap.

**The one thing that rule does NOT forbid:** stating the *necessary structural consequence* of a
verified fact, in the founder's own terms. *"Multi-visit treatments running with no tracking system
means some patients drop off without anyone noticing"* is a logical certainty derived from the audit —
not an invented pain point. What stays banned is asserting an unverified fact, or an unverified
emotional state ("you're probably frustrated," "I bet you have 40 of these").

**Why this matters:** a message that merely *reports* a researched fact reads as competent and lands
flat. A message that names the structural consequence, stated as the founder's problem rather than as
a research summary, lands. The bar: the first line creates instant recognition; the whole message
conveys real research depth; the net effect is the problem articulated more precisely than the founder
would say it themselves.

---

## 1 · Banned — fix before send, no exceptions

**Vocabulary.** leads · funnel · pipeline · conversion · ROI · lead generation · RevOps ·
infrastructure · "scale your practice" · growth · revenue leakage · patient journey · nurture ·
engagement · decision window · operational continuity · "gap" as a filler word
→ use **patients, enquiries, consult, booking, follow-up, walk-in**.

**Moves:**
- **"AI" as a lead.** Outcomes first; AI only if they ask how it works.
- **Any guaranteed patient or result number, ever.**
- **Rev-share at cold stage.**
- Superlatives (best / #1 / leading / guaranteed / proven).
- Generic compliments ("love your page!", "amazing work!").
- Stacked asks — one ask per message.
- Greeting filler ("hope you're well").
- Fabricated case studies, portfolios or results. **None exist. Say so if asked.**
- Hinglish or slang initiated by us — mirror only after they set the register.
- Presumptive psychology said out loud ("clinics like yours get burned by agencies").
- Cold "proposal" PDFs — send the 5-line WhatsApp breakdown instead.
- **Identical text to more than one person at the same clinic.** This happened — the same message went
  to all three Glow Clinic contacts on one day. In a market where they compare notes, it's a
  self-inflicted burn.
- **Asking for a call as the first ask.**
- **Any hook the doctor can rebut in one sentence.** See §2.
- **A mystery-shop finding from outside the clinic's own published hours.**

## 2 · The rebuttal test — run this first, before writing anything

For every candidate hook, write **the one sentence the doctor says back.** If a plausible rebuttal
exists, the hook does not ship.

| Hook | Rebuttal | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| "Your IG DM took an hour" | *We answer on WhatsApp.* | Kill |
| "You run 25 ads" | *And?* | Kill |
| "A 1★ from January" | *That patient was difficult.* | Kill |
| "We messaged at midnight, got a reply at 9am" | *We were closed.* | Kill |
| "89 one-star reviews, 0 owner replies" | — | **Ship** |
| "Priced a transplant Tue 2pm, went quiet, nobody chased in 9 days" | — | **Ship** |

This kills most of what research surfaces. **That is the intent, not a side effect.**

**Evidence bar:** a finding ships only if it is **a first-party test, OR a pattern across ≥2
independent instances.** Single-incident review quotes get argued with; patterns don't.

## 3 · Tone

- Founder-to-founder, personal account, first person singular. One observation, one ask. **30–125
  words.**
- Competence (the fact) before warmth (the identity line). Both required.
- Short sentences, plain words. If a smart 12-year-old wouldn't follow it, simplify.
- **No vague referents — ever.** Every phrase pointing at an action or consequence must name the
  concrete thing. "What I'd check first" is not acceptable unless what it refers to is stated.
- **Don't lead with an accusation.** Not *"I tested it, nobody replied."* Open with what's working,
  then frame the miss as a shared structural risk, never a verdict on their team.
- **The translation test — apply to every sentence:** *could an Indian clinic owner say this line, in
  these words, describing their own day?* If it sounds like a consultant's slide, rewrite it as a scene.

**Rejected:** *"Manual dependency creates operational inefficiency at scale."*
**Accepted:** *"That's the same person typing out that same 'hi, what treatment are you looking for'
reply to every single enquiry — and every minute spent on that is a minute not spent with the patient
actually sitting in your clinic."*

**Decline handling:** accept cleanly, zero push, no "can I ask why."

## 4 · The sequence — 4 touches, 2 channels

```
DAY 0 — IG DM: hook → identity (1 line) → tiny ask. No pitch.
   │
   ├── POSITIVE REPLY → STOP. Hand off to the post-reply motion (02, §6).
   │      ≤4 business hours. Nothing ships without a date or two named slots.
   │
   ├── SILENCE
   │      T2 (Day 2–3)   IG bump — one NEW fact, never "just bumping"
   │      T3 (Day 5–7)   WhatsApp, where publicly listed. New channel, same single ask.
   │      T4 (Day 10–12) The strongest remaining fact — usually the mystery-shop result
   │      → then park 60–90 days
   │
   └── DECLINE → accept cleanly, park, log.
```

**Every touch carries a new fact.** Never re-spend a fact already used in a prior touch or channel —
that's re-running a pitch that already went unanswered, not a fresh angle.

## 5 · Templates

**Day 0 DM** — hook → identity → tiny ask. No greeting filler.
```
Saw [specific verified thing — a comment, a timestamp, a public fact].
I'm [name], I work with clinics here on exactly this — [the problem in their words].
Want me to send the quick breakdown of what I found?
```

**Worked example:**
```
Saw your acne-scar Reel from last week — 6 comments asking about price and location, none answered yet.
I'm Tilak, I work with skin clinics here on exactly this — patients who asked and never got an answer.
Want me to send the quick breakdown of what I found?
```
*Two things this shows. "Leads" is banned — an earlier version of this exact example read "leads that
came in but never got a reply," which broke the rule three sections above it. And no hedging: "what's
likely happening" became "what I found," because a hedge in the CTA concedes you might have nothing.*
*Rebuttal test on this hook: "those get answered on WhatsApp" — plausible, so it only ships if the
comments are visibly unanswered AND there's no WhatsApp handoff on the post.*

**Silence bump (T2)**
```
One more thing I noticed — [new fact, not a repeat]. Happy to send the quick breakdown either way.
```

**Channel switch (T3)**
```
Tried you on Instagram — I'm [name], noticed [same hook, said differently].
Easier here? Happy to send the 2-line breakdown.
```

**WhatsApp bridge** — only after a reply, never cold.
```
Mind if I send this over on WhatsApp, easier to share the breakdown there — what's a good number?
```

**The meet ask** — drop-in first, call as the accommodation.
```
I'm around [area] Thursday — happy to swing by for ten minutes and show you what I found.
Or if that's more than you want to take on right now, a quick call works just as well.
```

**Decline**
```
Totally get it — happy to send the breakdown anytime if it's useful later, no pressure.
```

## 6 · Handling the six standard replies

**"How much?"** → don't price a cold DM; convert price-curiosity into the frame.
*"For the first pass — nothing. I want to look at the enquiries already sitting in your WhatsApp, and
if there's nothing there you owe nothing and I'll show you exactly where it stalled. Easier to walk
you through on WhatsApp — what's a good number?"*

**"We already have an agency."** → don't compete; occupy the gap they don't touch.
*"Makes sense — and I'm not touching ads or content, that's their lane. This is only the enquiries
that already came in and went quiet — the part nobody's usually assigned to. If your agency covers
that too, genuinely all good."*

**"Send me a proposal."** → a PDF to a cold prospect is death. Trade it for 5 lines.
*"Will do — it's short, 5 lines, specific to what I saw, not a brochure. Easier on WhatsApp so you can
read it between patients — what's a good number?"*

**"We tried this before, got burned."** → validate, differentiate on risk structure, never on skill.
*"Honestly the most common thing I hear from doctors here — usually someone promised a patient count
and delivered junk. That's exactly why the first pass is free, on your existing enquiries, and if
nothing real comes back you owe nothing and I'll tell you why. No numbers promised — I don't do that."*

**"Not now / after Diwali."** → accept cleanly, create one dated re-entry.
*"No problem at all. One thing — the enquiries sitting in your inbox from the last couple of months
lose value every week they sit. I'll check back in [named month]; if it's useful sooner, just reply."*

**"Sounds great!" (vague enthusiasm)** → convert to ONE concrete step immediately; it decays in 48h.
*"Glad it landed. Fastest way to make this real: mind if I move this to WhatsApp and send the 5-line
breakdown of what I'd do first? What's a good number?"*

## 7 · Self-check before showing any draft

- [ ] **Rebuttal test** — wrote the doctor's comeback; no plausible one exists. *(Do this first.)*
- [ ] **Admissibility** — if the hook rests on a mystery shop, the enquiry landed inside that clinic's
      own published Google hours.
- [ ] **CTA connects to the hook** — the ask follows from the observation. *(This has failed in the
      field: Glow Clinic's hook was DM response speed and the ask was about no-show rates.)*
- [ ] **Specificity** — at least one fact a stranger couldn't have written.
- [ ] **Source trace** — every claim points to a dated fact. Nothing marked *Potential / Possible /
      Unknown / ❓*.
- [ ] **Not already spent** — this fact hasn't been used on this clinic before.
- [ ] **Banned vocabulary sweep.**
- [ ] **Translation test** — could a clinic owner say this line about their own day?
- [ ] **No vague referents.**
- [ ] **Length** — under 125 words.
- [ ] **Tone** — founder-to-founder, not vendor-to-customer.
- [ ] **No guaranteed numbers.**
- [ ] **One ask only**, and it costs them as little as possible.
- [ ] **If drafting a batch: are any two messages sharing a skeleton?** Compare them side by side —
      convergence is invisible from inside one draft.

## 8 · The structural-variety rule

Four parallel agents once drafted DMs for 19 clinics with near-identical instructions. Two independently
converged on **the same opening skeleton for 11 of 19** — different clinics, different facts, different
agents. The same thing then happened to all 15 one-pagers: the same subhead appears verbatim in 10 of 10.

**Personalisation at the fact level does not prevent structural homogenisation.** Assign a deliberately
different opening shape per clinic. Seven named archetypes:

1. **Structural inevitability** — state the logical consequence of a verified fact
2. **Contrarian pattern-interrupt** — disarm the expected pitch first
3. **Compliment-then-pivot** — acknowledge a real strength, go downstream of it
4. **Review-quote / fact reframe** — their own patient's words, renamed as the real problem
5. **Mystery-shop story** — a small concrete thing that happened; let them draw the conclusion
6. **Blunt question** — no framing, the sharpest possible question
7. **Seen-not-answered mirror** — a verified non-response reflected back, no editorialising

*These are a craft discipline, not a tracked metric — at this volume no attribution is statistically
possible. Assign them so variety is deliberate rather than accidental.*
