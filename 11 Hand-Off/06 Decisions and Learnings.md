# DECISIONS AND LEARNINGS — what was tried, what happened, what was rejected

Read this before proposing anything that feels new. Several "good ideas" here have already been tried,
already been rejected, or already been logged and then not acted on.

---

## 1 · What was actually tried, and what happened

**16–21 Jul — Full-depth audits (45–90 min each) on 20 clinics before any outreach.**
→ 20 rich dossiers, a working segmentation framework, 7 cross-clinic patterns — and **zero DMs, zero
sends.** 0 of 20 audits captured the one field the Day-0 template actually required.
→ **Changed:** research inverted. Hook-first, ≤10 min, everything else post-reply.

**16–21 Jul — Mystery shops sent mostly 9 PM–1 AM to maximise "gap" findings.**
→ Clinics replied 9–11 AM next morning — **normal behaviour, not evidence.** Several logged "gaps"
were rebuttable in one sentence by the doctor.
→ **Changed:** midnight results banned as hooks. Later superseded by the published-hours rule.

**16–21 Jul — ICP scoring 0–10 as the targeting mechanism.**
→ All 20 clinics scored 7.5–10, including two with **no Instagram at all** and one whose front door
was excellent. **The score measured prestige, not winnability. A filter everyone passes filters
nothing.**
→ **Changed:** replaced by a kill-filter on reachability + verified leak evidence. **Scores are
historical metadata and may never be used as a reason to prioritise a clinic or spend more effort.**

**25–28 Jul — The first real sends. 6 touches.**
→ **0 replies.** Three avoidable defects: (1) the hook was a midnight shop, banned three days earlier,
sent anyway; (2) the hook was DM response speed and the CTA was an unrelated no-show pitch, with no
link between them — and that clinic's WhatsApp reply is 5 minutes, so the speed wedge was dead before
the message was written; (3) identical text went to three contacts at one clinic on the same day.
→ **Changed:** all three are now banned moves, and the CTA-connects-to-hook check is in the self-check.

**28 Jul – 5 Aug — The diagnostic one-pager as a cold touch-2/3 asset. ~25 clinics.**
→ **2 replies, 0 calls held, 0 pilots.** ~8% reply rate is *at or above* the 6–15% benchmark for
personalised audit-led outreach. **The document is not failing at attention. It is failing entirely at
conversion.** Neither responder engaged with what it asked for — one ran a credential check ("who are
you"), the other deflected to another inbox. Both are what people do when they're interested but can't
tell what they'd be buying.
→ Also found: all 15 documents converged on four repeating formulas (the same subhead appears verbatim
in 10 of 10), every one ran ~2× its own 250–350 word spec (529–703 words), and the CTA asked for a
free audit **when the document itself was the audit.**
→ **Changed:** stop optimising reply rate — it's fine. **Calls held is the metric.** Faceless-firm rule
suspended. Word ceiling enforced. Never send an asset without a date or two named slots.

**8 Aug — A 25-dossier batch written before any mystery shop.**
→ Every wedge tag provisional and untrustworthy. Three dossiers were written for clinics with no
founder named anywhere — a two-minute check sitting after the most expensive step.
→ **Changed:** the shop opens the batch. "No named decision-maker" became a hard kill at the start.

**11 Aug — Market-hours analysis across 171 clinics.**
→ **Saturday 6 PM is the highest published-open slot at 92%**, ahead of Tuesday 11 AM (89%) and well
ahead of Tuesday 2 PM (78%). 220 of 223 clinics are open Saturday.
→ **Changed:** published-hours admissibility replaced the old fixed window. **Untested — no Saturday
shop has been run yet.**

**13 Aug — Folder self-audit.**
→ Three internal audits had been written (22 Jul, 5 Aug, 11 Aug) and **almost none of their findings
had been merged into the governing documents.** The 22 Jul audit's five top fixes, each costed at 5–15
minutes, were still unapplied 22 days later. The tripwire installed 22 Jul had **never been filled in
once** while ~70–90 touches ran against a 100-touch limit.
→ **Changed:** everything merged. New standing rule — **an audit isn't finished until its accepted
findings are in the governing doc and its rejected ones are logged with a reason.**

## 2 · Settled decisions — don't re-litigate without new evidence

| Decision | Why |
|---|---|
| **Tiered personalisation** — ≤10-min research for Day 0, full audit only after a reply | 45–90 min/prospect produced 20 dossiers and 0 sends |
| **4 touches / 2 channels** (IG ×2 → WhatsApp ×2), then park 60–90 days | 2 touches ≈ invisible in this market |
| **Rev-share struck at cold stage** | Free + named guarantee + completion mechanism instead |
| **ICP prestige scores retired as a targeting tool** | 20/20 scored 7.5–10 including two unreachable clinics |
| **Mega founder-brands deferred to post-Case-Study #1** | Gatekeepers + incumbent agencies; client #1 odds are in founder-run mid-tier |
| **A warm reply outranks the send quota** | Sends are infinitely repeatable; a warm reply happens rarely and expires |
| **The heavy data-intake request moves to post-signature** | Asking an unsigned clinic for a CRM export, Meta access and patient data ends conversations |
| **Published-hours admissibility** replaces any fixed shopping window | Broader and stricter at once; costs nothing, the hours are already scraped |
| **Mystery shop cut from six tests to two** | Both fire from one message; the retired four were inadmissible, redundant, or expensive |
| **The shop opens the batch** | It's the only 24–72h async dependency and it was scheduled last |
| **Every hook carries a rebuttal column** | Generalises the midnight-shop lesson already paid for |
| **Evidence bar: first-party test OR ≥2 independent instances** | Single-incident review quotes get argued with |
| **Founder-unidentifiable clinics killed at the start** | 3 of 10 dossiers in one batch need never have been written |
| **Ledger shrunk to four numbers** — touches · replies · calls held · pilots | At this volume, copy-level attribution columns can't answer anything, which is why they went unfilled |
| **The faceless-firm rule is suspended** on one-pagers | A faceless case file invites exactly the "who are you?" question the warmest lead actually asked |

## 3 · ❌ Rejected — do not re-propose without new evidence

**The ₹7,500 paid diagnostic / small paid tripwire.** Proposed 5 Aug as a way to filter buyers and
produce revenue plus a case study fast. **Explicitly rejected 13 Aug.** The audit and strategy document
stay free; building and running the system is what's paid. *The diagnosis behind the proposal is still
accepted — the CTA gives away the audit and then asks for the audit. **Fix the ask, not the price.***

**Rev-share at cold stage.** Struck 22 Jul.

**ICP scores as a prioritisation argument.** Retired 22 Jul; still leaked into a briefing three weeks
later as the reason not to rush a clinic.

**A third IG-DM-only follow-up.** Touch 3+ moves to WhatsApp where a number is public.

**The IG→WhatsApp handoff wedge.** Killed after being led with twice — a cross-channel speed comparison
only matters if the slow channel is the one the business runs on, and here that's WhatsApp.

**Cold "proposal" PDFs.** Always the 5-line WhatsApp breakdown instead.

## 4 · The recurring failure modes — recognise these fast

**1 · The work that feels productive is the work with no exit.**
Research → enrich → send → wait is a closed loop that produces a full calendar and a flat pipeline.
It feels like progress because it's measurable and repeatable. **Sourcing has never been the
constraint, which is exactly what makes it the thing that gets done.** Named on 4 Aug; still the
sharpest diagnosis in the project. Applies equally to documentation work.

**2 · Findings get logged and then not acted on.**
The fast-responder problem was logged 21 Jul, flagged again 5 Aug, flagged a third time 11 Aug, and
was still happening on 13 Aug. Writing it down felt like fixing it.

**3 · Structural convergence is invisible from inside one document.**
Happened to the DMs (11 of 19 sharing an opening) and then to the one-pagers (10 of 10 sharing a
subhead), because each was written and reviewed alone. **Always review a batch side by side.**

**4 · "Provisional" graduates by use rather than by evidence.**
Several rules became de facto settled purely by being used in the field. **Field use is not evidence.**

**5 · A retired metric keeps steering decisions until it's deleted from the arguments, not just the
process.**

**6 · Treating a document's silence as evidence of absence.**
A folder audit concluded "zero mystery shops run" when twelve had been run three days earlier and
recorded in Notion. **The repo doesn't know what the repo doesn't record. Check the live source.**

## 5 · The validated reference bar for copy

Two messages have been confirmed as hitting the intended standard. Use them as the bar.

**Ministry of Skin, touch 2** — the shape: compliment on what already works → the specific miss, framed
as a shared structural risk rather than a team failure → the concrete mechanism (*the patient stops
looking elsewhere and waits, so a broken promise costs a committed patient, not just a reply*) → a CTA
naming exactly what's being offered.

**The Glow Clinic follow-up** — the translation test, passed:
> *"That's the same person typing out that same 'hi, what treatment are you looking for' reply to every
> single enquiry… every minute spent on that is a minute not spent with the patient actually sitting in
> your clinic."*

And the most disarming register anyone has produced here, from the one document that broke the
standard format — a named human sign-off plus:
> *"This is a read on public information and two test enquiries — not clinic data. Happy to be told
> where it's wrong."*
