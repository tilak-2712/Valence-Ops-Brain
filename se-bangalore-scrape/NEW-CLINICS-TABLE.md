## Data-quality notes

**Instagram follower counts** come from the clinic's own website via contact enrichment, so the
handle is tied to the right business rather than guessed by name. Two corrections were applied:

- Platform/vendor accounts scraped from website footers were **discarded**, not reported — `@tv`
  (Instagram's own account, 1.9M), `@wix` (a "built with Wix" badge, 880k, wrongly attached to two
  unrelated clinics), `@ekacarehq` (a healthtech vendor).
- Where a website links an Instagram account whose handle bears no relation to the clinic name, the
  row reads **"unconfirmed"** rather than showing a follower number that may belong to someone else.

**Exclusion matching** normalizes names and requires a shared *distinctive* (non-generic) word, so
"Dr. Pai Skin & Hair" is not wrongly matched to "Dr AG Skin & Hair". One clinic was excluded on its
Instagram handle rather than its name: "Vitals Hair and Skin Clinic" → `@vitalsklinic` → **Vitals
Klinic**, already on your list.

**Two clinics excluded that were NOT on the 80-name list** but are in Batch 1 per `CLAUDE.md` /
`MEMORY.md`: **Evenly Skin and Hair Clinic** and **Sapphire Skin & Aesthetics Clinic**. Worth
checking whether your 80-name list is complete against the Notion tracker.
