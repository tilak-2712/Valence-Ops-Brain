# Valence Ops — Bengaluru Elective Clinic Market Intelligence OS

**Purpose:** A living, accumulating dataset of elective clinic audits (aesthetic, dermatology, hair transplant, plastic surgery, dental) built from mystery-shop research, ICP scoring, and digital-presence analysis. Target: 500+ clinics. This is v1, built from the first 20 audits.

**Last updated:** July 2026 | **Clinics logged:** 20 / 500

---

## 1. How This Doc Works

Every new audit gets logged into the **Master Database** (Section 4) using the fixed schema in Section 6. Findings that repeat across 3+ clinics get promoted into **Cross-Clinic Patterns** (Section 3). The **Segmentation Framework** (Section 2) is the categorization layer used to decide *how* to pitch a clinic — it should only change when a genuinely new pattern emerges, not per-clinic.

---

## 2. Segmentation Framework — Two Independent Axes

Don't build one segment per clinic. Build templates on two axes that combine — this is what makes the system scale to 500 clinics without 500 different pitches.

### AXIS X — Buyer Archetype (who's actually deciding, and how to open the conversation)

| Archetype | Signal | Clinics in this dataset |
|---|---|---|
| **Mega Founder-Brand / Celebrity** | Founder's personal IG/press is the funnel (100K+ IG, celebrity/press positioning, multi-city) | DNA Skin Clinic, Dr. Karishma Aesthetics, Dr. Dixit, Anew Cosmetic, Ministry of Skin, The Glow Clinic |
| **Operator-Led** | Founder/co-founder is a non-clinician business operator; doctors are hired talent | Clinic Next Face (Ishmeet Singh), AvatarLuxe (Navin Koushik), The Glow Clinic (Rohit Reddy, Ops co-founder) |
| **Paid-Ads Performance Marketer** | Running double-digit active ads; thinks in CPL/CAC already | Ministry of Skin, Vtiara, Iridescent, Cozmo Blis, Skinology, Dr. Tina's |
| **Legacy / Zero-Digital** | No ads, thin or no social, Practo + referral only | Aura Cutisurg, Dr. Utkarsha's, Dr. Keshav's, Dr. Ritika Shanmugam, Dr. Juvita |
| **Multi-Partner Clinician** | Co-founded by 2-3 doctors, no single face, referral-network dependent | Dr. Sculpt (Medfin-referral) |

**Pitch logic by archetype:**
- Mega Founder-Brand → *"You've already won attention. The leak is between DM and chair."*
- Operator-Led → numbers/systems language, skip the trust-building preamble, fastest close.
- Paid-Ads Performance → cost-per-lost-lead math — they already understand ROAS.
- Legacy/Zero-Digital → modernization baseline, longer cycle, simplest possible framing.
- Multi-Partner Clinician → referral-routing and lead-attribution angle (who gets credit for the patient).

### AXIS Y — Clinical Vertical (case studies, SKU emphasis, treatment language)

| Vertical | Clinics | Emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| **Plastic/Cosmetic Surgery** (long decision cycle) | Dr. Sculpt, Karishma, Cozmo Blis, AvatarLuxe | Quote follow-up + consultation-recovery nurture over instant-response (decision cycle is weeks, not days) |
| **Hair Transplant** | Vtiara, Skinology, AvatarLuxe | Comparison-shopping nurture — ties to prior PromptOS finding that hair transplant is the most expensive/most-compared lead vertical |
| **Dermatology/Medspa** (skin, injectables, laser) | Ministry of Skin, DNA Skin, Dr. Dixit, Dr. Swetha's, Dr. Juvita, Iridescent, Dr. Tina's, The Glow Clinic, Dr. Ritika Shanmugam | Largest bucket — repeat-treatment logic makes retention/reactivation the strongest angle |
| **Dental/Multi-specialty** | Dr. Utkarsha's, Aura Cutisurg | Fold into Legacy template rather than building standalone |

A clinic's collateral = **1 buyer-archetype template + 1 vertical module.** As the dataset grows to 500, new clinics should slot into existing cells rather than generating new templates — if a clinic doesn't fit any cell cleanly, that's the signal a new pattern is emerging.

## 3. Cross-Clinic Patterns (findings that repeat across 3+ audits)

1. **Acquisition is solved, conversion is leaking — in 18 of 20 clinics.** "No-show recovery," "quote/consultation follow-up," and "dormant lead reactivation" appear as missing layers almost universally. This validates NSR and Dead Pipeline Reactivation as the right lead SKUs regardless of segment.
2. **Late-night enquiries die.** Nearly every mystery-shop test sent between 9 PM–1 AM got zero response until the next morning, often 9-11 AM (Aura Cutisurg, Dr. Utkarsha's, Vtiara, Dr. Dixit, Cozmo Blis, Iridescent, Anew). After-hours capture is a near-universal gap.
3. **Ad spend and conversion infra are uncorrelated.** Clinics spending on 15-44 active ads (Ministry of Skin, Iridescent, Cozmo Blis, Vtiara, Skinology) show the *same* missing-follow-up pattern as clinics with zero ads. Paid spend doesn't predict operational maturity — don't assume high-ad-spend clinics have their conversion layer figured out.
4. **Solo-doctor bottleneck is an explicit, named complaint** in at least 2 audits (Dr. Juvita: "the entire clinic depends on one single doctor"; DNA Skin: "difficulty accessing Dr. Priyanka because of demand"). This is a distinct pain-point worth its own talking point for single-founder clinics — the problem isn't marketing, it's founder capacity.
5. **Instagram DM automation lags WhatsApp automation almost everywhere.** Multiple clinics have WhatsApp auto-replies but no equivalent IG DM flow (Ministry of Skin, Karishma, Anew, DNA Skin). IG-to-WhatsApp handoff is a specific, repeatable gap to name in outreach.
6. **Only one clinic (Skinology Centre) passed the front-door test cleanly** — 1-minute human response with a qualifying question. Every other audit found either silence, a generic auto-reply, or a manual delay of 15 min to several hours. This is the rarest finding in the dataset — worth flagging any time a new audit matches it.
7. **Where WhatsApp automation already exists (Anew, Ministry of Skin, Karishma), it stops at greeting/acknowledgement** — none show automated qualification, treatment segmentation, or scoring. This is an "upgrade the depth" pitch, not an "install from scratch" pitch, and should be flagged separately per clinic so outreach doesn't pitch something that already half-exists.
