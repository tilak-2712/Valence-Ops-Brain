### Recording method

- Stand up, walk while talking. Sitting and reading a screen is what produced the recital.
- Record three takes. **Keep the second.** First is stiff, third is over-rehearsed.
- Leave the imperfections in — a pause, a "sorry, what I mean is", a restarted sentence. Those are proof a human recorded it in one pass, which is the whole reason this beats the email and the deck that got ignored.
- Never three balanced items in a row. If you catch yourself listing, stop at one example.
- Don't perform the last line. If it needs performance, cut it.

## Why engineered this way

- **Roshan is the trust anchor, not the audit fact.** He sanctioned both the number and the in-person meeting — line one answers "who is this and why is it legitimate."
- **The text carries zero pitch** — if both messages carry content, it reads as pursuit on a new channel (the thing that got the email/deck/IG ignored). The announced voice note converts the stack from "two pitches" into "one invited listen."
- **"You've heard from us a few times, so I won't repeat any of it"** — names the elephant once, lightly, inside the voice note.
- **The compliment is the 29-min reply** — verified, specific, doubles as proof of real work. Generic compliments are banned; this one is earned.
- **One ask:** the Whitefield visit — the exact next step Roshan himself proposed.
