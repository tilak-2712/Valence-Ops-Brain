The systems we would build

Seven systems, each closing one specific gap between an enquiry and a completed visit. The first three, Qualification, Booking and No-Show Recovery, are the core of the pipeline. The other four are typically sequenced once those are holding steady. Exact order to be agreed together once your answers to the form are back.

All of them run across the channels enquiries already arrive on: WhatsApp, whether the patient comes from the Google listing, an ad, or messages the number directly, Instagram and Messenger. Messages are built in English by default. Other languages are supported, and confirmed with you before anything is built.

03

How it works together

These systems are built to connect into one pipeline, so no enquiry falls through a gap between stages.

  • A lead qualified by the bot books directly into a held slot through the Booking System, or is passed to the front desk to confirm one.
  • A call that arrives when the desk is closed is answered by Voice Reception, and reaches the front desk as the same structured summary a message would.
  • A booked appointment flows into No-Show Recovery if the patient does not show up.
  • A completed visit flows into Review Generation.
  • A lead that goes quiet at any stage becomes a candidate for Reactivation later, rather than simply disappearing.

Your team does not need to learn a new system. They keep working the way they already do, responding when a patient replies, while the infrastructure behind each stage runs in the background. The one thing they add is marking down what happens offline, so the record matches what actually happened.
