03

What this looks like as the clinic grows

Every step on the previous page needs someone free at the exact moment it needs doing.

On a normal day, that works. On a full day, or when a treatment runs long, or when a case comes in unplanned, the tasks that wait are the ones with nobody sitting in front of you. The reminder. The follow-up. The call to the patient who did not arrive.

That is not a problem with the process. It is what happens when the work grows faster than the hours available to do it. And with more going into advertising, the work grows.

The choice this creates

At double the enquiries, the same steps still have to happen. Today the only way to do that is to add a fourth person at the front desk, and then a fifth.

The other option is to take the repeatable work off the three people you already have. The reminders, the confirmations, the first replies, the chasing, the typing into the CRM. None of it needs judgement. All of it needs doing, every time, without fail.

That is what this proposal is for.

What we would build

Three systems, switched on one at a time.

01
Enquiry handling, on every channel
Enquiries reach you in several places. WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook Messenger and your website form. Each is watched separately today. This brings them into one place.
WHAT IT DOES
  • Every channel lands in one view, so nothing sits unread in an app nobody opened that day
  • Replies to a new enquiry straight away, at any hour, on any day
  • Asks what treatment they want, what their concern is, and when they are free
  • Sends the booking request to your front desk with the full conversation attached
  • Hands over to a person the moment anyone at the desk replies in the thread
WHERE IT STOPS
  • It does not confirm the appointment. Your rep checks availability and confirms
  • It does not share prices and it does not give medical advice. If you later want it to share standard package prices, that is switched on only when you ask for it
02
CRM entry and a weekly report
Your CRM fills itself in. Once a week you get one page of numbers nobody had to prepare.
WHAT IT DOES
  • Writes every enquiry, reply, booking and appointment result into your CRM. No typing
  • Records how long each enquiry waited before someone answered
  • One weekly page: enquiries received and from where, how fast they were answered, consultations booked, consultations attended
WHY IT MATTERS
  • You can see conversion week by week instead of estimating it at month end
  • Everything is written into your CRM, not held by us. The record stays with Aurilueur
WHERE IT STOPS
  • How much we can write back depends on what your CRM allows. We check that with you before the build begins

03
Reminders, missed appointments and free slots
Every appointment confirmed and reminded. Cancellations known early enough to refill the slot. Missed appointments picked up the same day.
WHAT IT DOES
  • Confirms the appointment when it is booked, and reminds the patient the day before
  • The patient can reply to the reminder. They confirm, or they say they need to move it
  • Because they reply, you know about a cancellation while the slot can still be filled
  • Marks who has confirmed, and gives the desk a short list each morning of who has not
  • When a patient does not arrive, raises a call for your rep the same day
  • Where a patient is part-way through a course of treatment, offers them the next available times rather than chasing them
  • Keeps a list of patients waiting for an earlier slot, messages them when one frees up, and tells the desk who said yes
  • When the day has to be rearranged, sends the messages to everyone affected and collects the replies
WHERE IT STOPS
  • It does not decide the schedule and it does not book anyone. Your rep picks the times and confirms
  • Every sequence has a set number of attempts, then stops
Not part of this

Two more systems are worth looking at once these three are running and there are numbers to judge them by. Following up on quotes that go quiet, and keeping weight-loss programmes on track between months. Neither is included or priced here.

We would also not manage availability. That changes daily and depends on your doctors, and the decision should stay with your team.

05

What your team does instead

Nobody at the front desk is replaced. It is worth saying that plainly.

Every appointment you book, including each session inside a treatment course, needs a confirmation sent, a reminder sent, someone checking for a reply, and a note in the CRM. Four small actions, repeated for every appointment, every week.

The system takes all of them. What reaches a person is the exception. The patient who wants to move an appointment. The one who did not arrive. The one taking a slot that opened. Far fewer actions, and each one worth more.

FIRST HOUR
OF THE DAY
The calls. Anyone still unconfirmed for today. Patients who missed a session. Patients who were quoted a few days ago and have not come back. These are worth the most and are the first to slip when the day gets busy.
THROUGH
THE DAY
The patients in front of them. Check-in, payments, keeping doctors and rooms coordinated, and the consultation itself, where someone is deciding on a course of treatment. Your best reviews are about your people being attentive. That is the part to protect.
END OF
THE DAY
Tomorrow. Who needs moving, which slots are still open, and anything the system has flagged.

What stops: first replies, confirmations, reminders, chasing for a reply, and typing any of it into the CRM.

Anyone can take over a conversation at any time. Replying in a thread stops the system on that patient straight away. No double messages, nothing to switch off first.
