## What this changes

- **The no-show claim needs locating.** He calls no-shows a frequent problem, yet ~90% of quoted patients
  show for treatment and somebody chases a no-show immediately. The loss is therefore probably at the
  **consultation** stage, not the treatment stage. Worth asking directly rather than assuming.
- **Cancelled slots are the clean gap.** No waitlist, no callback list, "manually when possible". That is a
  concrete, uncontested hole a system fills, and it sits next to the pain he already named.
- **Confirms the absorption frame, does not weaken it.** Four-plus follow-ups, immediate no-show chasing and
  manual quote follow-up all describe a clinic doing the right things by hand. The argument stays *same
  output, less labour, headroom to grow ad spend* — never "you are losing leads". See `taste-n-judgement.md`.
- **His stated goal is thin.** "Trying to get to connect with enquiries" and "More lead conversion" are two
  short lines from a man who needed three chases to fill a five-minute form. Read as low written engagement,
  not low interest — he shows up to conversations. Carry the specifics into the call; do not expect the
  document to do the work.
