# LinkedIn Sales Navigator Personas — ValenceOps ICP

Built from the 80-clinic list, real title patterns already found in `Pre-outbound-research/` dossiers (e.g. "Founder / Aesthetic Dermatologist," "Founder / Plastic & Cosmetic Surgeon"), the buyer profile in `personalized-outbound-v2.md`, and the qualification rules in `wedge-signal-entry.md`. Use these to build Sales Nav searches and to sanity-check who the real decision-maker is before a connection request goes out.

Across almost every clinic in this list, there is one buyer, not a buying committee: the founder-doctor. `wedge-signal-entry.md` hard-kills any clinic needing committee sign-off — so every persona below assumes a single-decision-maker practice.

---

## 1. The Solo Founder-Dermatologist (core persona — most of the list)

**Who this is:** the doctor whose name is on the door. Runs skin/hair/laser or skin-and-hair clinics — Dr. Keshav's, Dr. Ritika Shanmugam, Dr. Dixit, Dr. Swetha's, Dr. Juvita, Dr. Tina's, Dr. Rai's, Dr. Sowmya's, and most of the single-doctor clinics on the list fall here.

**LinkedIn title patterns actually observed:** "Founder / Dermatologist," "Founder / Aesthetic Dermatologist," "Founder, [Clinic Name]," "Cosmetologist, Dermatologist & Trichologist," "Dermatologist | Founder | Cosmetologist."

**Sales Navigator filters:**
- Title (keyword, not standardized field): `Founder OR Dermatologist OR "Cosmetic Dermatologist" OR Trichologist`
- Industry: Medical Practice, Hospital & Health Care, Cosmetics, Wellness & Fitness
- Geography: Bengaluru / Bangalore Urban
- Company headcount: 1–10, 11–50
- Seniority: Owner, Founder, Partner
- Boolean profile-text add-on: `"skin clinic" OR "hair clinic" OR "dermatology clinic"`

**Buyer psychology (from `personalized-outbound-v2.md`):** doctor-first identity — thinks in patients/consults/bookings, not funnels or leads. Likely already burned by a marketing agency; lead with de-risking, never a guaranteed-patient-count number. WhatsApp is where the real work happens; LinkedIn/Instagram is where they're findable and where credibility gets read, not where the deal gets done.

**Reachability note:** several founders in this exact persona have no usable Instagram (Dr. Utkarsha's, Dr. Keshav's) — LinkedIn is sometimes the *only* channel, not a backup. Confirm before assuming IG-first.

## 2. The Cosmetic / Aesthetic Physician-Founder

**Who this is:** runs an aesthetics-branded practice rather than a straight dermatology one — Ministry of Skin, Iridescent Aesthetics, AvatarLuxe, Aesthetica Veda, Aarha AesthetiQ, Feather Touch Academy, Koza Aesthetic Care (5-branch chain — check for a non-founder ops layer here).

**Title patterns:** "Founder / Aesthetic Physician," "Medical Director," "Aesthetic Dermatologist," "Cosmetologist & Aesthetic Physician."

**Sales Navigator filters:**
- Title: `Founder OR "Medical Director" OR "Aesthetic Physician" OR Cosmetologist`
- Industry: Cosmetics, Medical Practice, Wellness & Fitness
- Keyword: `aesthetics OR "aesthetic clinic" OR "medical aesthetics"`
- Company headcount: 1–10, 11–50 (bump to 51–200 for chains like Koza)

**Buyer psychology delta from persona 1:** more likely to already run paid social/Meta ads and think of themselves as brand-builders, not just clinicians — so competence-proof needs to be sharper (a specific audit finding, not a generic compliment) since their feed is already full of agencies pitching them. Multi-branch chains (Koza, Praba's Vcare — excluded) often have a non-doctor ops layer between you and the founder; check for a General Manager / Operations Head title before assuming the doctor is directly reachable.

## 3. The Plastic / Cosmetic Surgeon-Founder

**Who this is:** Dr. Sculpt Aesthetic Clinic, Dr. Karishma Aesthetics, Cozmo Blis, Anew Cosmetic Clinic, Venkat Center for Aesthetic Health, Gejje's Marvella. Often multi-founder (Dr. Sculpt has three named founders) and higher average ticket size (body contouring, liposuction, surgical procedures).

**Title patterns:** "Founder / Plastic & Cosmetic Surgeon," "Founder / Plastic Surgeon," "MS, MCh — Plastic Surgery."

**Sales Navigator filters:**
- Title: `"Plastic Surgeon" OR "Cosmetic Surgeon" OR Founder`
- Industry: Medical Practice, Hospital & Health Care
- Keyword: `"plastic surgery" OR liposuction OR "body contouring"`
- Note: multi-founder practices need one Sales Nav search per named founder, not one search per clinic — check each partner's activity/reachability separately (this is the mega-founder-brand deferral tension flagged in `MEMORY.md` §5 for Karishma, Sculpt, Anew, DNA Skin — these are lower-priority sends regardless of how easy they are to find on LinkedIn).

**Buyer psychology delta:** higher ticket size means quote-decay is a stronger wedge than dead-lead reactivation — see `wedge-signal-entry.md` §3 routing table row "High-ticket quote given, never chased." Surgical-procedure founders are also more likely to be the mega-founder-brand park tier — check follower count and outbound volume before prioritizing.

## 4. The Hair Transplant Surgeon-Founder

**Who this is:** Vtiara Hair & Skin, Neo Follicle, CosMediQ, Contour Cosmetic Clinic, Haircosmos International, VIDA Skin & Hair Transplant, Ridhi's Skin and Hair Transplant, Advanced Gro Hair & Glo Skin.

**Title patterns:** "Founder / Hair Transplant Surgeon," "Trichologist," "Hair Restoration Specialist."

**Sales Navigator filters:**
- Title: `"Hair Transplant" OR Trichologist OR "Hair Restoration"`
- Industry: Medical Practice, Health, Wellness & Fitness
- Keyword: `"hair transplant" OR FUE OR FUT`

**Buyer psychology delta:** category has an unusually high rate of aggressive, guaranteed-result marketing from competitors — the "never name a guaranteed number" rule matters even more here, since these founders have seen the most scam-adjacent pitches in the category. Lean hardest on a specific, screenshot-provable audit finding rather than any promise language.

## 5. The Founder-Dentist / Multi-Speciality Dental

**Who this is:** Chisel Dental, Sky Dental, Reneu Dental Studio, Aspen Dental Care, Smiley House, Smile Xpressions, The Dental Axis, Amaya Dental Clinic, Dr Tanisha's Emerge Dental Studio, Aesthete Lifestyle Dentistry. Small Bites (kids-only chain) likely has a different, more operationally-minded buyer than solo dental founders.

**Title patterns:** "Founder / Dental Surgeon," "Chief Dentist," "BDS, MDS — [Specialty]," "Dental Director."

**Sales Navigator filters:**
- Title: `Founder OR "Chief Dentist" OR "Dental Director" OR "Dental Surgeon"`
- Industry: Medical Practice, Hospital & Health Care
- Keyword: `dental OR dentistry OR orthodontics OR implants`
- Company headcount: 1–10, 11–50

**Buyer psychology delta:** dental founders think in recall/follow-up appointments (cleanings, implant stages, aligner check-ins) rather than single-visit consults — the multi-visit-treatment dead-patient-reactivation wedge (`wedge-signal-entry.md` row: "multi-session treatments... 12+ months operating") applies especially cleanly here. Speak in "recall," "appointment," and "case" rather than "lead" or "funnel."

## 6. The Access-Workaround Persona: Clinic Head / Lead Doctor / Ops Manager (non-founder)

**Who this is:** the reachable person when the founder is gatekept — a named "Clinic Head," "Lead Dermatologist," or front-desk/ops contact at larger or multi-doctor practices (Clinic Next Face has both a Founder and a separately named Clinic Head and Lead Dermatologist).

**Title patterns:** "Clinic Head," "Lead Dermatologist," "Center Manager," "Practice Manager," "Operations Head."

**Sales Navigator filters:**
- Title: `"Clinic Head" OR "Practice Manager" OR "Center Manager" OR "Operations Head" OR "Lead Dermatologist"`
- Same industry/geography filters as persona 1.

**How to use this persona, per `wedge-signal-entry.md` §1.2:** front-desk-only reachability is a *park* signal, not a kill — route around it via a Practo doctor listing, the clinic's website team page, or this exact LinkedIn title search, rather than disqualifying the clinic. This persona is a routing tool to reach persona 1–5, not usually the actual economic buyer — confirm who signs off before pitching them directly.

## 7. Mega-Founder-Brand / Industry KOL (park tier, not a cold-send priority)

**Who this is:** founders with large personal followings and an established public brand — Dr. Priyanka Reddy-style "Industry KOL" profiles, DNA Skin, and the three surgeon-founders flagged in persona 3. Per `MEMORY.md` §5, ValenceOps is explicitly deferring this tier until Case Study #1 exists.

**Title patterns:** "Founder," "Industry KOL," often paired with a large stated following in the headline or featured section.

**Sales Navigator filters:** same as the underlying specialty (persona 1–4), but filter/sort by **follower count** and posting frequency where Sales Nav surfaces it — that's the signal that routes a profile into this tier instead of a direct-send tier.

**Why this persona exists separately:** longer sales cycle, harder access, likely gatekept by a team — strong underlying fit but wrong sequencing for a first client. If one of these shows up in a search, tag and park rather than adding to this week's send list.

## How to use this doc in Sales Navigator

1. Pick the persona matching the clinic category you're prospecting (map from the categories list in the clinic tracker).
2. Build the search with the title + industry + geography filters above, then narrow by company headcount to exclude hospital-chain-scale accounts (this ICP is small, founder-run practices — `wedge-signal-entry.md` hard-kills anything needing committee sign-off).
3. Before sending a connection request, run the clinic through the disqualification framework (`wedge-signal-entry.md` §1) and confirm reachability against what's already known in `MEMORY.md` §5 (some founders are IG/WhatsApp-only, some route through LinkedIn only) — don't treat a LinkedIn match alone as qualification.
4. Once a real profile is found for a clinic already in the Notion tracker, add the LinkedIn URL to that clinic's row rather than duplicating it here.

**What's not in this doc:** no specific named LinkedIn profiles beyond the handful already confirmed in `Pre-outbound-research/` — that's per-clinic research, not persona-building, and belongs in each clinic's dossier.
