# ValenceOps — Event Snapshot
**Compiled 2026-09-02. Covers everything from project start (mid-July) through the latest dated file in the repo.**

Scope note: this is an **events-and-outcomes log only** — what happened, with whom, and what resulted. It deliberately excludes drafting doctrine, taste/judgment calls, copywriting rules, and sales technique (those live in `taste-n-judgement.md`, `personalized-outbound-v2.md`, `wedge-signal-entry.md`, `OUTBOUND_MEMORY.md` §2–5). Epistemic tags (`confirmed` / `provisional` / `hypothesis`) are carried through from source docs where stated.

---

## 1. Foundation phase (16–22 Jul 2026)

- **16–21 Jul:** Full-depth research audits (45–90 min each) run on 20 Bangalore clinics before any outreach began. Produced 20 research dossiers and a segmentation framework — but **zero drafted DMs, zero sends**, and 0/20 audits actually captured the hook type the Day-0 template needed.
- **16–21 Jul:** Mystery shops in this first pass were sent mostly 9 PM–1 AM to try to surface "gaps." Clinics replied 9–11 AM the next morning — normal business behavior, not evidence of a problem. Several "gap" findings from this period were later judged rebuttable in one sentence by the doctor.
- **16–21 Jul:** ICP scoring (0–10) tested as a targeting method. All 20 clinics scored 7.5–10, including two clinics with no usable Instagram. The score was measuring prestige, not winnability, and was retired as a targeting tool on **22 Jul**.
- **22 Jul — `OUTBOUND_SYSTEM_AUDIT.md` produced.** Finding: the pipeline was terminating at research, never reaching outreach. This audit is the source of the tiered-research model, the kill-filter, the 4-touch/2-channel sequence, and the 100-touch/45-day tripwire (installed this date, requiring re-examination of the clinics-only bet if 100 touches or 45 days pass with zero pilots).

## 2. First real sends (25–28 Jul 2026)

- **25 Jul (reported retroactively):** First-ever Day-0 sends — **Cozmo Blis** ×3 on WhatsApp. **Outcome: 0/3 replies.** The lead hook was a midnight enquiry-response gap, which the published-hours rule (adopted later) would have disqualified.
- **28 Jul:** **The Glow Clinic** ×3 on Instagram, sent to three separate points of contact (Dr. Varshini, Rohit Reddy, Dr. Sindhiya Annamma). **Outcome: 0/3 replies.** All three messages were identical text to different people at the same clinic — flagged afterward as a specific, avoidable failure (a market where people compare notes).
- **First 6 logged touches across both clinics: 0 replies, 0 calls, 0 pilots.**

## 3. Diagnostic one-pager campaign (28 Jul – 5 Aug 2026)

- **28 Jul – 5 Aug:** The "Revenue Diagnostic" one-pager sent cold as touch 2/3 to roughly 25 clinics.
- **Outcome: 2 replies, 0 calls held, 0 pilots.** ~8% reply rate (at/above the 6–15% benchmark cited for this outreach type — the document was not failing to get attention).
  - **Sapphire Skin & Aesthetics** replied — said the observation was good, asked for a company profile.
  - **Aesthetica Veda** replied — saw the one-pager, handed over a decision-maker's email address.
- Neither responder engaged with what was actually being offered; both replies read as "interested but unclear what's being sold."
- A separate document-level review found all 15 one-pagers produced in this window converged on the same four structural formulas (headline shape, subhead, "what we're not saying" line, close), each running ~2× the intended word count.

## 4. Batch 7 mystery-shop run (8–16 Aug 2026)

- **8 Aug:** 25 full research dossiers ("Batch 7") written before any mystery shop was run against them — **0/25 shopped** at the time of writing. Three of the ten highest-priority dossiers were later found to be for clinics with no identifiable founder at all.
- **10–11 Aug:** Batch 2 (12 clinics) actually mystery-shopped, mostly on WhatsApp, business hours. Results (per Notion, still current as of this snapshot):
  - **Wedge confirmed (5):** Dr. Priya's Skin & Hair Clinic (doctor personally fielding cold enquiries on her own phone, 1-min reply then 24h+ silence), Project Skin (bot double-fired, then 24h+ silence against 14 months of continuous ad spend), Akera Health (19-hour reply gap against 54 simultaneous ad creatives — "strongest evidence in the batch"), Derma Solutions (60-second reply that deflected to a website link with no qualification), Haircosmos International (2h26min to answer "what treatments do you offer" on a ₹49,999 procedure).
  - **Wedge confirmed, contact not yet made (3 more):** Dermatonik, Ara Skin Clinic, VIDA Skin & Hair Transplant, Gejje's Marvella, Vitals Klinic — each shopped, evidence mixed-to-strong.
  - **Test running, do-not-contact (clock-based):** Krity 360 (quote-decay + IG-persistence clocks from 10 Aug), Theory of Skin (quote-decay + abandoned-slot clock from 10 Aug — the only clinic in the batch where a human actually called back, then never retried after a missed call).
  - **Blocked on founder identification:** Project Skin, Akera Health, Haircosmos — evidence sat unused pending an MCA/ownership lookup.
- **13 Aug — Folder self-audit.** Three standing audits (22 Jul, 5 Aug, 11 Aug) were reconciled against the live docs; most of their recommended fixes had never actually been applied (e.g., rev-share still present in the drafting skill 22 days after being "struck"). This produced a wave of rule consolidations (published-hours admissibility replacing the old business-hours window, mystery-shop scope cut from 6 tests to 2, `SEND_LOG.csv` restructured, evidence-bar rules tightened) — process changes, not sales events, so not detailed further here.
- **13 Aug — Touch-count correction.** Reading Notion directly (rather than the repo) revealed actual cumulative touches were **~70–90 of the 100-touch tripwire**, not the ~40–65 estimated from repo files alone — the tripwire was much closer to firing than believed.

## 5. Batch 9 — new-cohort qualification & mystery shop (15–20 Aug 2026)

A ~58-clinic scrape (adjacent aesthetic/derm clinics, referred to elsewhere in the repo as "Batch 9") went through a separate qualification pass in this window, parallel to Batch 7's shop run. This did not surface in the first version of this snapshot because the two folders it lives in (`05 Prospects/Mystery Shop 2026-08-15/`, `05 Prospects/Verify 2026-08-18/`) had only been directory-listed, not read.

- **15 Aug:** Mystery shops actually run (WhatsApp, hair-fall/acne enquiries) against 10 clinics: Aestheticaa (Dr. Madhulika), Divine Aesthetics, Body Science The Aesthetic Clinic, Dr. Sood Aesthetics, Elixir Advanced Aesthetics, Kavana Dermo Glamm, Maya, Moon Aesthetic, Regenique Dermatology and Aesthetics, Sun Light Skin Clinic — screenshots on file, findings not yet written up into wedge form for most of them. Three of these (Elixir, Regenique, Maya) are the same clinics that later show "Wedge confirmed" with no DM sent yet in the Notion Batch 2 table (§ above) — this is their evidence source.
- **18 Aug — Tilak personally disqualified 6 clinics** from the pool by direct call, logged with reasons: Aesthetics Plus/Dr. Surindher (plastic surgery, corporate entity — outside ICP), Rock Aesthetics Clinic (16 lifetime reviews — volume too thin), The New Body Perfect & Smile Lounge (weight-loss led — outside ICP), Krian Healthcare (general practitioner — outside ICP), Facial Aesthetics Center/Dr. Nisha Shetty (maxillofacial/dental surgical practice — outside ICP), Dr. Anil Abraham's Skin & Hair Clinic (mega founder brand — 24 reviews against 67k followers, credibility mismatch).
- **18–20 Aug — Ad/review/Instagram verification run across the remaining 52 clinics.** By the 18 Aug resume checkpoint: 57/58 Google-review pulls verified, 53/58 Google-ads checks verified (5 flagged), 57/58 Meta-ads checks verified (1 flagged), only 30/56 Instagram handles confirmed (26 still pending). Newly confirmed as running live Meta ads in this pass — each an upgrade in priority: Rejuvaderm, The Derma Theory, Maira Wellness Clinic, Masa aesthetics. Derma Elite was downgraded (keyword-matched to unrelated advertisers, no real ads).
- **19 Aug — Enquiry-flow qualification cut applied: 31 of 52 qualify.** Split into Tier A — live paid demand (16 clinics, dr_shettys_cosmetic_centre through skinwork), Tier B — 400+ reviews with no ads (11 clinics), Tier C — large Instagram only, weakest evidence (4 clinics). 15 clinics excluded outright for no demonstrable enquiry flow; 6 more sit borderline (300–399 reviews).
- **State as of 25 Aug** (`state.json` last touched): verification work still open on the outstanding Instagram handles; no mystery-shop write-up, DM draft, or Notion row exists yet for the bulk of this cohort — only the three clinics named above (Elixir, Regenique, Maya) have crossed into Notion Batch 2.

## 7. SkinFit engagement (16 Aug – 30 Aug 2026)

This is the most developed deal in the project's history and the only one to reach a formal proposal/SOW stage.

- **16 Aug (pre-meeting research):** Confirmed via ZoomInfo + Instagram + Meta Ad Library that Gino Tharakan (the contact) is Associate Creative Director at Nester's Hub — the agency that built/hosts SkinFit's site — and that Nester's Hub's director has "Building @skinfitwellness.in" in his own bio (an undisclosed stake in the clinic). Also confirmed SkinFit had gone live on Meta ads (~3 Aug, 20 active creatives, ₹499/₹1,499 tripwire offers), directly contradicting a "no paid campaigns" claim already sent to Gino in earlier material.
- **18 Aug — First-ever in-person client meeting held**, at Koramangala, with Gino Tharakan. This is the first meeting the project has held with any prospect. Produced the SkinFit tech handoff and technical brief documents that followed.
- **20 Aug — Audit path closed.** Gino said an audit was not possible soon and asked instead for a proposal with costs. A 55-question self-report questionnaire was built to substitute for the audit; reviewed and found to answer only 2 of the 8 original audit objectives — including missing the headline number the audit was meant to produce (Meta conversation count vs. their manual daily count).
- **21 Aug — Pricing/build-cost model set.** ₹20,000 setup / ₹40,000 per month, though at this point pricing only 4 of what became 6 proposed system components.
- **22 Aug — First Proposal & SOW draft produced, then reviewed.** Review found it had dropped the "booked appointment" capability — the strongest promise in material already sent to Gino — and added an unsubstantiated "no PII stored" compliance claim. Flagged for correction before sending.
- **22 Aug — Pricing decisions finalized:** Sarvam confirmed as the conversational-AI vendor; Instagram/Messenger DM confirmed in scope; hosting costs folded into the retainer rather than billed separately.
- **27 Aug — Proposal v2** produced, restyled to match the visual format of the earlier Enquiry Handling Review document per direct instruction.
- **29–30 Aug — Proposal iterated three more times** ("Enquiry System Strategy" → "Patient Conversion System" → "Patient Conversion and Intelligence System" → final "Patient Conversion Proposal"), each a new PDF/HTML pass.
- **Undated (most recent) — meeting held with Drish Israni and Gino Tharakan.** The system agreed on stayed close to what had already been planned, with a number of changes plus one new creative concept developed in the meeting. **The proposal for that finalized system has been sent** to SkinFit. Reply/response status not yet known.

## 8. RUA Skin & Hair Center (25–26 Aug 2026)

- **25 Aug:** Pre-call research pass found the existing MASTER dossier's qualification ("established presence, 80+97 reviews across two branches") was wrong — a full 84-review census put RUA's oldest Google review at **15 Oct 2025**, making the clinic **10 months old**, which fails the project's own 12-month minimum-age qualifier. The wedge originally assigned (dead-lead reactivation) was one a clinic this young structurally cannot support, and was reassigned.
- **26 Aug:** A "Patient Communications Proposal" and separate "Scope of Work" document were produced for RUA.
- **Undated (most recent) — online meeting held with RUA's founder.** At the end of the meeting, the general proposal was sent along with two intake forms — one for RUA's operations team, one for the founder himself. **No response received yet; two follow-ups have been sent since.**

## 9. Legal and business registration

- **Valence Ops is a sole proprietorship**, proprietor S Tilak, commenced 1 Jun 2026 — not a Pvt Ltd or LLP.
- **Held:** Udyam registration (UDYAM-KR-03-0707297, Micro/Services, registered 4 Jun 2026) and Karnataka Shops & Establishments registration (25/37/CE/0063/2026, registered 31 Jul 2026, valid to 31 Dec 2030, 3 employees declared).
- **GST — deliberately not being registered, by decision (confirmed 2 Sep).** The RCM concern that originally motivated filing is resolved: every tool/vendor currently in use charges IGST directly, which removes the reverse-charge liability that would have required registration. No GST filing planned for now.
- **Confirmed 2 Sep: legal status is fully resolved** — the address-mismatch and documentation defects flagged after the 20 Aug registration review have been cleared.

## 10. Corrections and reversals worth keeping as events

- **13 Aug:** A prior internal claim that the ~150 non-Notion clinics represented "sourcing sprawl" was retracted after Tilak clarified they were deliberately scraped and parked as future-batch inventory, not abandoned work.
- **13 Aug:** A prior claim that Batch 7/Batch 2 had "zero mystery shops run" was found false — Notion showed 12 clinics had in fact been shopped on 10–11 Aug; the repo simply hadn't recorded it.
- **23 Aug:** Two Day-0 drafts (Maya Medi Spa, Regenique) were caught before sending — each contained a specific, checkable ad-spend claim (an ad campaign, a start date, a "number printed on the ad") that did not exist in any source data. Neither clinic has ever run the ad described. Caught in review, not sent.
