**The single most informative artefact in this dossier is a negative review and its reply.**

**1★, 2026-08-02:** *"They charged me Rs 750 consultation fee only to tell me that it their closing time and they will do treatment next day. I just needed a skin tag removal... So basically no consultation was done. And still I was asked to pay Rs 750... And they kept calling again n again."*

Owner reply (abridged): *"Your appointment was scheduled for 1:30 pm, which we confirmed by message at 1:14 pm. When you had not arrived, we called at 2:21 pm, during which you informed us that you were on your way. We also reminded you that the clinic would be closing at 5:00 pm that day. As the closing time approached, we called again at 4:41 pm. You arrived at 4:55 pm, five minutes before closing..."* — followed by a clinical explanation of why a consultation is medically necessary before skin-tag removal.

**Why this matters for wedge diagnosis.** That reply reconstructs a confirmation message and two chase calls with exact timestamps. A clinic that can do that is already running an appointment-confirmation and follow-up process, and logging it. The complaint itself is a patient who arrived 3.5 hours late — the clinic's chasing worked; the patient's timekeeping didn't. **This is evidence of a functioning process, not a broken one.**

Corroborating: multiple 5★ reviews independently describe proactive post-treatment follow-up calls — *"she even called me for follow up... The staff also called me later to check up on my health."*

Owner reply rate is high across both positive and negative reviews, and replies are substantive and signed "Team Krity 360."
