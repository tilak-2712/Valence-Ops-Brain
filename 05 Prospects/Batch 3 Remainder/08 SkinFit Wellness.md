## Digital Presence — identity resolution (revised twice; final position below)

The IG bio claims two locations: "Koramangala | Richmond Road." A GBP search for "SkinFit Wellness Richmond Road Bangalore" did **not** surface a distinct Richmond Road listing — it resolved back to the same Koramangala profile. A separate, similarly-named business, **"Skinnfit Medspa"** (double-n), was found in J.P. Nagar with its own domain (`skinnfit.in`), own phone, and 13 reviews.

**This conclusion changed twice. Full reasoning, so it can be judged independently:**

- **First read:** possibly the same brand's second location, on name similarity alone.
- **Second read (recorded here initially):** unrelated businesses — a same-name collision, based on the different spelling and different domain.
- **Final read — the second conclusion was wrong and is reversed.** Two independent signals point to a genuine connection:
  1. **SkinFit Wellness's own Instagram bio links to `skinnfit.in`** — the *other* entity's domain. This is a clinic-authored link, not an outside inference.
  2. **A 2023 Skinnfit Medspa review references "Dr. Ruby" by name** as the person escalated to — the same founder name as this clinic.

**Current position: likely related — an earlier or affiliated venture of the same founder — but the exact relationship is unresolved.** It is **not** confirmation of the claimed Richmond Road branch: the addresses don't match (J.P. Nagar ≠ Richmond Road) and the GBP website fields differ. Treat SkinFit Wellness as **single-location** until confirmed otherwise; the Richmond Road claim remains **unconfirmed**.

**Disclosed for completeness:** the single negative review on the Skinnfit Medspa listing (2023, no owner reply) alleges non-payment to a service provider and states the matter was raised with "Dr. Ruby" without resolution. This is an **unverified B2B payment-dispute allegation from one reviewer three years ago**, on a 13-review listing whose relationship to this clinic is itself unconfirmed. It carries very little weight and should not drive any decision — recorded only because omitting an inconvenient finding would corrupt the audit.
