**Pipeline:** `compass/crawler-google-places` · `apify/facebook-ads-scraper` · `scrapesage/google-ads-transparency-scraper` · `apify/instagram-profile-scraper` · `harvestapi/linkedin-profile-scraper` · `apify/website-content-crawler`

## Methodology

Every review count and rating below comes from a direct Google Business Profile pull (up to 120 reviews/clinic, newest-first); every complaint quoted was actually read; every phone/email/social link was extracted from the business's own listing or crawled site, not guessed; founders are checked against a real LinkedIn profile URL where one was supplied.

- **Batch 1 (13 clinics):** 9 of 13 founders confirmed via LinkedIn search; 3 (CosMediQ, Nishka, ProSkincare Esthetics) not re-verified — the search actor hit its own daily free-tier cap.
- **Batch 2 (10 clinics):** user supplied direct LinkedIn profile URLs, so all 8 personal profiles pulled cleanly with no rate-limit issue. Several leads turned out to be multi-branch chains — noted per clinic.

> **Negative rate** = share of the up-to-120 newest reviews pulled per clinic rated 1–2★.
> **Owner replies** = reply rate among the negative reviews actually sampled, not full history.
