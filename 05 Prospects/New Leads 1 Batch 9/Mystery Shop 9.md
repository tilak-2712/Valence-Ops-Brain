# Mystery Shop 9 — Batch 9 Clinics (SHOP-QUEUE positions 11–20)

**Evidence status:** WhatsApp screenshots supplied for all ten Batch 9 clinics. The initial enquiries were sent on Saturday, 15 August 2026; screenshots also show replies on the following day where applicable. This report records only the behaviour visible in those captures. “No reply visible” means no reply appears before the screenshot was taken, not that the clinic never replied. Commercial context is drawn separately from `batch 9.md` (research dated 15 August 2026) and is not presented as a mystery-shop finding.

**Timing convention:** A same-minute or roughly 1-minute reply may be automation or a staff member pasting a template; the screenshots cannot distinguish those mechanisms reliably. This report therefore treats replies delayed by more than **5 minutes** as a visible manual-response/handoff gap, while describing faster messages as **immediate** rather than automatically calling them automated.

## Evaluation lens — Lead Capture & Automation

Each clinic is assessed across the same live enquiry path:

1. **First-response speed** — whether a prospect receives a timely acknowledgement.
2. **Capture and qualification** — whether the clinic learns the treatment need, concern, or readiness before it acts.
3. **Conversation continuity** — whether the thread progresses from acknowledgement through an answer and a next step.
4. **Conversion** — whether the clinic gives a useful next step, price context, or consultation pathway.
5. **Channel fit** — whether the clinic answers a text enquiry in text before requiring a call or clinic visit.

## Cross-Clinic Findings

| Pattern | Clinics | Operational meaning |
|---|---|---|
| No meaningful reply visible after a specific enquiry | Maya, Elixir | Maya has no visible acknowledgement; Elixir acknowledges instantly but never advances the specific hair-loss conversation. Both need ownership and progression, not merely a generic greeting. |
| Next-day first response | Divine, Kavana Dermo Glamm, Aestheticaa | A high-intent prospect waits roughly 16–20 hours for a first visible answer. Divine and Aestheticaa then leave follow-up questions unresolved or fragmented; Kavana redirects to calling. |
| Strong early automation, incomplete human conversion | Dr. Sood, Moon, Body Science | These clinics prove the front door can work. The common missing link is structured human handoff that turns collected information into a confirmed slot. |
| Price is disclosed, booking is not completed | Moon, REGENIQUE, Dr. Sood, Sun Light, Body Science, Aestheticaa | Fee transparency exists more often than a real conversion process. Offer specific times, capture the choice, and confirm the booking rather than ending after the number. |
| Text enquiry pushed to a visit/call before basic information is resolved | Sun Light, Moon, Kavana Dermo Glamm | The clinic may appropriately need an assessment to recommend treatment, but the thread should still answer the prospect’s basic question, capture intent, and offer a controlled next step in chat. |
| No visible re-engagement after a warm price enquiry or partial answer | Divine, Moon, REGENIQUE, Dr. Sood, Sun Light, Aestheticaa | None of these visible threads shows a staff follow-up that asks whether the prospect would like to reserve a time after they request price or information. |

## Priority Actions

1. **Maya, Elixir, Divine:** establish a non-negotiable immediate acknowledgement plus human-response SLA. Maya needs basic coverage; Elixir needs an acknowledgement that understands the stated intent; Divine needs same-day ownership.
2. **Kavana Dermo Glamm:** replace the delayed “call us back” recovery message with a text-capable hair-fall intake and scheduled callback workflow.
3. **Moon, Sun Light, REGENIQUE, Aestheticaa:** combine the frequently requested facts — treatment category, indicative price/duration or consultation fee, and next step — in one clear response so prospects do not have to ask twice.
4. **Dr. Sood and Body Science:** retain the strong automation, but connect it to selectable appointment slots and a confirmed-booking record. They are the closest to a scalable qualified-lead journey.
5. **All ten:** track WhatsApp enquiry → acknowledged → qualified → price/fee disclosed → slot offered → slot confirmed → attended. A greeting or a treatment list should not be counted as conversion.
