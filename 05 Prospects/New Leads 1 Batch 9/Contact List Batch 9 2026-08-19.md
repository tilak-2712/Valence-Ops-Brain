1. **Send on IG or email, never the shopped WhatsApp number.** Standing practice from Dr. Priya's Notion row.

   On **22 Aug** the four clinics that quoted a price (Moon, Body Science, Dr. Sood)
   gain a second, stronger fact — *"and still nothing a week later"* — usable at touch 2.

   one observation and an ask that costs them nothing — the live demo number they can message themselves, never
