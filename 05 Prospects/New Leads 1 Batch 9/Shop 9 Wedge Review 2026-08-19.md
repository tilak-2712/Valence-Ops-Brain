### 3.1 Two fail the rebuttal test (§0.1) — do not ship as written

| Report wedge | The sentence the doctor says back | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Regenique — "**~44-minute** manual-response gap" | *"We answered inside an hour, on a Saturday evening."* | **Kill** |
| Body Science — "**~28-minute** manual handoff" | *"We replied in half an hour and offered him Monday."* | **Kill** |

**The bar already exists in Tilak's own hand.** Notion Batch 2, Ara Skin Clinic: *"18 minutes in-hours is not
damning on its own. Wedge is thin as it stands."* Gejje's Marvella at **56 minutes** only became a wedge because
of the bot-does-more-than-the-person contrast plus the silence after it — never the 56 minutes.

**So the report's 5-minute threshold is miscalibrated for this market.** Working number: under ~30 minutes is
not a gap, it is a competent desk. Between 30 min and ~2 hours it is a wedge only in combination with something
else. Over 12 hours it stands alone.

### 3.2 The speed wedge is doing all the work again

Seven of ten recommended wedges are a response-gap variant: Divine, Regenique, Dr. Sood, Maya, Elixir,
Body Science, Kavana, Aestheticaa. §2.5, standing warning, flagged three separate times:

> *"The routing table below has 15 rows and one of them is doing all the work."*

The report also reaches for the softened reframe §2.5 specifically names — *"speed is not the wedge, the gap
is…"* — on Moon, Sun Light and Elixir. That concedes the checkable fact and swaps in an abstraction the founder
cannot verify.

### 3.3 Vocabulary that must not reach a DM

"SLA", "service-level window", "escalation", "response ownership", "conversion structure", "channel fit",
"intent-aware auto-reply", "monitored automation-to-staff handoff". Fine as internal shorthand, fatal in copy —
`MEMORY.md`: *could this line be said, in these words, by an Indian clinic owner describing their own day?*
