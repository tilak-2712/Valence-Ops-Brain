## One correction to the 15 Aug headline

**Hairline International is filed `SPENDING NOW` on evidence that does not support it.** Its
recency (6 days) comes from `HAIRLINE DIAGNOSTICS AND HEALTH CARE PRIVATE LIMITED` — an advertiser
the same file marks **Unconfirmed** and a different legal entity. Its own confirmed Meta page
(`Hairline International Hair & Skin Clinic`) holds **1 lifetime ad, 0 in 30 days, no date**.

Cause: `consolidate.py` lines 101–109 compute `recency` from `g["last"]` before checking
`g_verdict`, so an unconfirmed advertiser's date can promote a clinic to *Spending now*. Hairline is
the only row where this changes the verdict (checked all 16). **Correct read: 15 spending now, 13
unresolved.** Fix the script rather than the JSON, per the handoff note on hand-editing `results.json`.

## What these four columns do and do not license

- The 15 defensible *Spending now* clinics are the only ones where **“you are paying for enquiries
  right now”** is a dated fact (`CLAUDE.md` §5). For the other 27 it is an assumption.
- Ad **count is not spend**, and no ad library publishes budget. Enquiry volume is not derivable
  from any of this. See `Session Handoff 2026-08-15.md` §6.
- **No wedge can be assigned from this data** — `wedge-signal-entry.md` §4 requires a mystery shop
  for response behaviour. This is a shop-ordering input, not a send list.
- **Repo presence ≠ contacted.** None of these 42 are in Notion.
