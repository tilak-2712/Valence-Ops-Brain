**2026-07-16 → 2026-07-21 — Tried:** ICP scoring 0–10 as the targeting mechanism.

**2026-08-11 — Insight (no experiment):** across the 171 aesthetic/derm clinics in the SE Bangalore scrape, **Saturday 6pm is the highest published-open slot at 92%** — ahead of Tuesday 11am (89%) and well ahead of Tuesday 2pm (78%). 220 of 223 clinics with published hours are open Saturday. ## 2026-08-25 — "Established presence" is not a substitute for the oldest-review date

**Tried:** Deep-research pass on RUA Skin & Hair Center (2 branches) ahead of a sales call, starting
from the batch-3 aesthetic MASTER dossier.

**Happened:** The MASTER passed hard kill #2 with the reasoning *"established presence, 80+97 reviews
across two branches, multi-year footprint implied."* A full 84-review census put the oldest Google
review at **2025-10-15** — the clinic is **10 months old**, and fails the kill. §1.1 #2 names the
oldest-review date as the test and marks it scrapable, so this was always determinable; it was
inferred from review *volume* instead of *dates*. Knock-on: the assigned wedge (dead-lead
reactivation) was the one wedge a sub-12-month clinic structurally cannot support.

**Changed:**
1. **Review volume never implies age. Pull the oldest review date.** A newest-sorted pull capped
   below the total review count cannot see the floor — RUA's 60-newest bottomed out at 2026-02-21,
   24 reviews short of the real 2025-10-15 start. Cap ≥ total, or the age check is worthless.
2. **Log the monthly review histogram, not just the count.** RUA's spiked to 28 in Feb 2026 and fell
   back to 4–6. That shape is a question to ask, never an assertion.
3. **Owner-reply rate per branch, not per brand.** RUA: 93% at the flagship, 32% at the new branch.
   Averaged, that finding disappears entirely.
4. Site defects worth grepping on every clinic from now on, all found here in one pass and all
   verifiable by the founder in under a minute: placeholder `tel:` links (`+1234567890` on all 5
   pages), `og:url` pointing at a non-resolving domain, per-channel WhatsApp numbers landing in
   different branch inboxes, and a GBP booking link absent from the website.
