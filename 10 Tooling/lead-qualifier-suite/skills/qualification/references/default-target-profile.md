# Default Target Profile

Use this profile unless the user provides an override.

## Worked-with Comparables

- Tier 1: Smartlead, Instantly, HeyReach, Aimfox, Clay, Amplemarket, Apollo, CompanyEnrich
- Tier 2: Lindy, Trigify, Better Contact, Reactin, Linxa

## Strong Fit

- Clean B2B SaaS products selling to revenue, sales, growth, outbound, demand-gen, RevOps, or GTM teams
- Cold email, deliverability, inbox infrastructure, and outbound sequencing
- B2B lead generation, prospecting, contact data, enrichment, and sales intelligence
- LinkedIn automation, multichannel outbound, AI SDRs, and agentic sales workflows
- GTM workflow software where the buyer is a sales or marketing team inside another business

## Adjacent Fit

- RevOps, CRM sync, sales workflow automation, and pipeline operations software
- B2B workflow automation and agent platforms with a clear business-team buyer
- Browser automation or data infrastructure that obviously supports GTM or outbound use cases
- AI-native B2B SaaS tools that still map clearly to sales, growth, or revenue teams

## Explicit Non-Focus

- Ecommerce brands, stores, and retail-first tools
- Legal tech, compliance law platforms, contract-law products, and law-firm software
- Hospitals, clinics, medical providers, patient tools, and healthcare operations software
- B2C apps, consumer subscriptions, creator tools, gaming, social, dating, or prosumer products
- Agencies, services businesses, marketplaces, job boards, media properties, communities, and directories
- Chatbot-first, customer-support-first, customer-service-first, and contact-center-first businesses
- Crypto, gambling, real-estate brokerages, education providers, travel businesses, and restaurants
- Generic AI tools with no clear B2B SaaS workflow buyer

## Labels

- `priority_now`: direct outbound, GTM, prospecting, enrichment, deliverability, or AI-sales fit with a clear B2B SaaS buyer
- `good_fit`: strong adjacent workflow, agent, RevOps, or B2B AI-tool fit with a clear business-team buyer
- `maybe`: B2B SaaS but weaker overlap, broader workflow fit, or less direct outbound relevance
- `skip`: off-profile vertical, non-SaaS model, B2C or service-heavy motion, or insufficient evidence of clean B2B SaaS fit

Use AI judgment from researched evidence. Do not use hard-coded domain labels.
