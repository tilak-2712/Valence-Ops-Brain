# Aurilueur Esthetic Clinic — Post-Meeting Intake Form

For Dr. Shabeeb, following the offline meeting. Trimmed to exclude everything already confirmed
verbally (CRM = Neodove, 15–20 enquiries/day, ~35–40% conversion, ~90% show-up-to-treatment post-quote,
no-shows named as the main pain point, 3 front desk/ops staff, 4 doctors + Shabeeb, positive-but-noncommittal
on marketing). Every question below is chosen because the answer changes a system, a scope decision, or the
commercial proposal — not because it's generically useful to know.

Format key: **Checkbox** = multi-select · **Multiple choice** = single-select · **Paragraph** = open text.
The grey line under each question is the Google Form "description" field — sets expectations on depth/format,
not a leading example.

## Format rationale (why options vs. open text)

| # | Question | Format | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1, 3, 4, 7, 9 | Channel mix, after-hours, cadence, no-show, quote follow-up | Options | Behavior falls into a small, known set of buckets — options are faster for him and cleaner for us to tabulate |
| 2, 8 | Marketing outlook, slot-filling | Options | Same, but 8 is checkbox since more than one method can be true at once |
| 5, 6, 10, 11 | Inbox/number, appointment flow, goals, success | Paragraph | Answers are compound or genuinely open-ended — forcing options would lose the nuance that actually drives the proposal (e.g. "one shared inbox" vs. "each staff member's own number" changes the WhatsApp integration entirely) |
