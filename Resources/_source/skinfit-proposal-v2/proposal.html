Where this picks up

The Enquiry Handling Review shared separately with SkinFit Wellness set out what happens to an enquiry today: replies go out late, callbacks often land at a moment that doesn’t suit the patient, and there is no structured follow-up or no-show recovery running yet. The no-cost audit proposed in that review is the actual next step, confirming exactly where enquiries go before anything below is built.

This document sets out what we would build once that is confirmed: what each system does, how they connect, roughly how long setup takes, and what it costs. It is not a commitment to build everything at once. The order things get switched on is something we would agree together once the audit is done.

The systems we would build

Six systems, each closing one specific gap between an enquiry and a completed visit. The first three, Qualification, Booking and No-Show Recovery, are the core of the pipeline. The other three are typically sequenced once those are holding steady. Exact order to be agreed together once the audit findings are in.

01
AI Lead Qualification Bot
Answers a new enquiry immediately, on any channel, at any hour, and captures the concern before anyone at the desk has to pick up the phone.
WHAT IT DOES

  • Structured qualification flow: requirement, service, budget range, availability, urgency

03

How it works together

These systems are built to connect into one pipeline, so no enquiry falls through a gap between stages.

  • A lead qualified by the bot books directly into a held slot through the Booking System, or is passed to a rep to confirm one.
  • A booked appointment flows into No-Show Recovery if the patient does not show up.
  • A completed visit flows into Review Generation.
  • A lead that goes quiet at any stage becomes a candidate for Reactivation later, rather than simply disappearing.

Your team does not need to learn a new system to benefit from this. They keep working the way they already do, responding when a patient replies, while the infrastructure behind each stage runs in the background. The one thing they add is marking down what happens offline, a walk-in, a verbal confirmation, a note taken at the desk, so the record matches what actually happened.
