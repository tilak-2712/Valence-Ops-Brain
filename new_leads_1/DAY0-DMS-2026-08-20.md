## What was wrong with v1

All three drafts broke the same three rules, and `MEMORY.md` names every one of them.

| # | v1 did this | The rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Opened *"I messaged you and nothing came back."* | **"Don't lead a finding with 'I tested it, nobody did' — open with what's working, then frame the miss as a shared/structural risk, not a verdict on their team."** All three opened with the verdict. |
| 2 | Closed on *"want me to send the breakdown?"* — three times | **"No vague referents — ever. Hard default."** Breakdown of *what*? The CTA has to name the concrete thing being offered. |
| 3 | Middle line was *"the gap between a message landing and someone picking it up"* | `personalized-outbound-v2.md`: **"A message that merely reports a researched fact reads as competent and lands flat."** v1 reported the fact and stopped. There was no cost, no mechanism, nothing the doctor could feel. |

**The validated shape, which v1 skipped entirely:**
> compliment on what already works → the specific miss as a shared risk → **the concrete mechanism (what it actually costs, as a chain)** → the scale fact → CTA naming exactly what is offered.

The mechanism is the part that makes it land. It is the part v1 had none of.
